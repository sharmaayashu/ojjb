01 THE BAND

A big sound,
built together.

18 musicians.
One unmistakable sound.

The Ottawa Junior Jazz Band (OJJB) is an 18-piece big band consisting of Grade 7, 8, 9, and 10 students from the Ottawa region. In its 24th season, OJJB provides a platform for students to expand on their musical experiences and discover the joy of big-band music.

OJJB has earned 24 consecutive Gold Standards at the Capital Region Music Festival and a Gold Standard at every performance at Musicfest Canada's National Competition. The band has appeared at the Ottawa International Jazz Festival and Montreal Jazz Festival, and travelled to Dallas in 2009 to perform at the University of North Texas Jazz Festival.

The band has shared the stage with jazz artists Mark Ferguson, Nick Dyson, and Mike Tremblay. Students aspiring to join OJJB Auditions take place every June.

THE PERSON BEHIND THE MUSIC
MAKING MUSIC IN OTTAWAOJJB · MUSICAL DIRECTOR

MEET THE DIRECTOR

Mandar
Gumaste.

B.Mus., B.Ed. · Musical Director

Mandar is an intermediate music teacher at Longfields-Davidson Heights Secondary School in Barrhaven. He is the former music teacher at A. Lorne Cassidy E.S. and Fielding Drive P.S., and began his teaching career at New Central P.S. in Oakville.

He has been a band director for 23 years and, in 2011, received the Ottawa-Carleton District School Board's Fine Arts Recognition Award for Intermediate Music. Ensembles under his direction have performed at a high level of excellence at the Capital Region Music Festival and Musicfest Canada Nationals, achieving many Gold Awards.

His school jazz band has earned 16 consecutive Gold Awards at the Capital Region Music Festival and five Most Outstanding Jazz Ensemble Awards at the Regionals. He has been the founder and director of OJJB since its inception.
